# Growth Strategy Memo: Onboarding Experiment & Business Impact

**To:** Head of Growth, PesaPap  
**From:** Lead Growth Analyst  
**Date:** September 2026  
**Subject:** Onboarding Flow Experiment (Variant B) Rollout Decision  

---

### 1. Result
Variant B (simplified sign-up screen) yielded a statistically significant uplift in onboarding conversion compared to Variant A (control).
* **Control (Group A) Conversion:** 10.7%
* **Variant B Conversion:** 14.1%
* **Relative Lift:** +31.8%
* **Statistical Significance:** $p$-value $< 0.01$ ($\chi^2 = 8.12$, 95% CI for difference: $[+1.1\%, +5.7\%]$).

### 2. Impact
Assuming a baseline signup volume of **10,000 new users per month**:
* **Additional Converted Users:** $+340$ newly onboarded active users monthly.
* **Financial Value Creation:** At an average customer 90-day transaction volume of KES 4,500, rolling out Variant B dynamically adds **~KES 1.53M in annualized transactional value** to PesaPap’s bottom line.

### 3. Recommendation
**Roll out Variant B (simplified sign-up screen) to 100% of incoming users immediately.**

### 4. Caveats
1. **Downstream Quality:** Higher onboarding conversion must be monitored to ensure user retention and average transaction quality do not degrade over a 60-day horizon.
2. **Transaction Validity:** Data validation revealed a minority of transactions logged prior to registered signup dates, requiring data engineering updates to fix timestamp synchronization.
